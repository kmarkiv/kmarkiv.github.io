01 / Point of view

Thoughtful AI is not defined by what a system can do.

It is defined by what becomes possible for the people using it.

That shifts the questions I ask. Not only “Is the model accurate?” but “Whose goals does it serve?” “Will it work here?” and “Does it leave people with more agency, understanding, and room to act?”

02 / How I work

From lived context to lasting value.

Three commitments connect my work across education, civic participation, and the Global South.

A

Listen before building

Start with aspirations, constraints, and knowledge already present in a community—not with a technology looking for a use.

B

Design for reflection

Use conversation and AI to help people learn, explore choices, and make judgments of their own.

C

Stay for the long term

Evaluate beyond the demo: in real settings, over time, and at the scale where infrastructure and institutions matter.
