About

Practical AI for learning, reflection, and participation

I design and study practical, low-cost AI systems that support learning, reflection, and civic participation. My work is particularly concerned with settings where infrastructure, connectivity, and resources shape what useful technology can look like.

I completed my PhD in Human-Computer Interaction at Carnegie Mellon University, advised by Prof. Amy Ogan and Prof. Tim Brown. My dissertation evaluated low-cost, chat-based teacher learning systems that supported 400+ teachers and 10,000+ students in rural Côte d’Ivoire. Read the thesis draft.
